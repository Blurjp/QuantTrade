# Oil Storage Signal

> **Detection Method:** Tank shadow analysis on SAR/high-res optical
> **Applied To:** [[cushing]]

## How It Works

Oil tank fill levels at Cushing, OK are estimated from tank shadow analysis. Rising inventories suggest oversupply (bearish WTI), falling inventories suggest tightness (bullish WTI).

## Key Metric

- **Tank fill level:** estimated from shadow length ratios
